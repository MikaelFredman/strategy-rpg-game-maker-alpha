# Community guidelines

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and behavior, not the worth of another person.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, or publish private information.
- Do not distribute illegal material, malware, executables, or content you lack permission to share.
- Keep bug reports focused; use Discussions for broader design conversations.
- Moderators may edit labels, move conversations, hide unsafe attachments, lock repeated arguments, or remove abusive content.

Repeated or serious violations may result in restricted participation.
